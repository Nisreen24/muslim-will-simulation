Dev notes

  • The seat is reserved but not Used: Used stays 1, Available drops to 0 — this is what prevents over-inviting.
  • Actions on a pending row: Copy link, Resend, Cancel. Cancel frees the seat instantly and invalidates the link server-side.
  • Invitation auto-expires after 14 days → moves to the Expired state (State 5) and frees the seat.
  • Only one active invitation per email per subscription.
  • Delete-and-resend flow: Cancel frees the seat instantly, so the owner can immediately send a fresh invitation — to the same email or a different one.

Dev notes

  • Expired invites do not reserve the seat — Available goes back up.
  • Actions: Resend (creates a fresh invitation → back to State 2) or Remove (clears the row).
  • Opening an expired/cancelled link shows: “This invitation is no longer valid. Ask your family member to send a new one.”
